BIRTHDAY SURPRISE — QUICK TEST GUIDE
====================================

1. Open index.html in a browser. The landing page should show "A little surprise awaits."
2. Click Continue. The birthday reveal should appear with a blue confetti burst.
3. Scroll to the five sisters. Click each card to reveal its individual message;
   click again to close it.
4. Read Alya's Russian greeting and its English translation.
5. Click "Open Your Final Message" to reveal the final note.
6. Click Celebrate! to launch another confetti burst.
7. Test on a narrow/mobile-sized browser window and with keyboard navigation.

The portraits are inline SVG illustrations, so there are no external image
dependencies. The optional Google Fonts import improves typography; system
font fallbacks are provided.
For GitHub Pages, publish the repository's root folder and keep the three site
files together. Use a neutral repository name if you want to keep the surprise.
